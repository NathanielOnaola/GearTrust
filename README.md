# GearTrust
Shared trust layer across independent rental marketplace
It would be great if there was a shared trust layer across independent rental marketplace  

What if your rental history could travel with you

Let's say you own a car worth $15,000. You don't use it every day, so renting it out is a good way to earn extra income

Someone contacts you to rent it for a weekend 

You want the money but you have questions 

► Who is this person
► Have they rented expensive equipment before
► Can you trust them to return your camera in good condition

The person renting it also has concerns

► How much will the deposit be
► What happens if the owner claims the camera is damaged
► How can they prove they have a good rental history

Now, imagine that person has rented equipment from five different businesses, returned everything on time and had no major issues

When they approach you, how do they prove that history

They might have screenshots, old receipts or references from previous rental businesses. You could contact those businesses individually but that takes time

And if they use a different rental platform, their previous activity may not be visible to you

I think there's an opportunity here 🤔

The idea: a rental history people can carry between businesses

My proposal is a shared system for equipment rentals where people can build a verifiable rental history and use it across participating rental businesses

Think of cars, power tools, generators, cameras, laptops, projectors and other professional equipment

Today, a person's rental experience can be scattered across different businesses and platforms

A business may know that someone has rented from them several times but another business has no easy way to confirm that history

I'd definitely like to see that change

A renter should be able to build a record of their rental activity and present relevant parts of it when approaching another participating business

The equipment owner should also have a record
Renters should be able to check whether a business has a history of fair transactions, clear agreements and properly handled deposits

Both sides should have something to prove

➡︎ How would it work in actual sense

Let's use the car rental example

1) Every rental has a proper record

When a rental is agreed, the business records the equipment involved, rental period, payment, deposit, responsibilities and condition of the car before it leaves

The renter and owner both agree to the terms

This gives both parties something they can refer to if a disagreement comes up later

2) Completed rentals build a person's history

When the equipment is returned, the business confirms the outcome of the rental

► Was it returned on time
► Was the equipment returned in the agreed condition
► Was there a dispute
► If there was one, how was it resolved

The business that handled the rental issues a signed record of the outcome

Over time, the renter builds a history backed by businesses that actually handled those transactions

This matters because people shouldn't be able to create their own positive rental records or give themselves ratings

3) The renter can use that history elsewhere

Now, that same renter approaches another camera rental business

Instead of starting from zero, they can share relevant records from previous rentals

The new business can check who issued those records and whether they are valid

The renter doesn't have to expose every detail of their previous transactions

They should be able to choose what information to share, while the receiving business gets enough evidence to make an informed decision

The same principle applies to rental businesses. A person looking to rent equipment should have a way to check a business's record before handing over money or a deposit

4) Deposits and agreements get better protection

The system can also support rental agreements and deposit handling through smart contracts.

For example, a car rental costs $50-$100 and a refundable deposit $200 (depends)

The agreed terms are recorded before payment. Once the rental ends and both parties confirm that everything is in order, the deposit can be released

If there's a damage claim, the money shouldn't automatically go to either party

The agreement should provide a dispute process, with evidence and human review where necessary

A smart contract cannot inspect a camera or decide who is telling the truth. It can, however, enforce the payment conditions both parties agreed to

➡︎ Why should this be onchain?

This part deserves a detail attention

A rental platform can already store customer records, manage deposits and collect reviews. We don't need blockchain to do those things

The challenge comes when different rental businesses need to recognise records issued outside their own platforms

If every business keeps its own database, a renter's history remains scattered

If one company controls the entire system, everyone has to depend on that company to maintain and verify the records.

An onchain system could give participating businesses a shared way to issue and verify rental records without relying on one rental platform to control the entire history

For example, a verified rental business can issue a signed record after a completed transaction

The record can be checked by another participating business, even if the two businesses use different platforms

Personal information, identity documents, photographs and detailed inspection reports should remain offchain

Only the necessary proofs and verification records need to be anchored onchain

There's also an important condition here, the businesses issuing these records must be verified. Putting false information on a blockchain doesn't make it true

And if all the businesses involved are happy to trust one central operator, a conventional database may be simpler

The onchain approach becomes more useful when independent businesses want to share and verify records without handing control to one company

➡︎ Who would use this

I'd start with equipment rentals

1) Individual equipment owners: People who own cars, power tools, generators, cameras, laptops, projectors and other professional equipment and want to rent them out with more confidence

2) Rental businesses: Companies that want to assess customers using verifiable rental history rather than relying only on personal judgement

3) Freelancers and professionals: People who regularly rent equipment for projects and would benefit from building a history they can present to different businesses

4) Rental platforms: Existing platforms that want to integrate shared verification without having to build their own reputation system from scratch

The system doesn't need to become another marketplace where everyone lists equipment for rent

Existing rental businesses can continue managing their customers, listings and bookings

They simply connect to a shared system for rental records and verification
_______________________________

➡︎ What needs to be handled carefully

There are a few things I wouldn't want us to overlook

A renter shouldn't be permanently labelled because of one disagreement

People need a way to challenge inaccurate records, and resolved disputes should be represented fairly

Businesses must also earn trust before their records can be accepted

Otherwise, someone could create a fake rental company and issue positive records to themselves

Privacy matters too. A person's entire rental history shouldn't be publicly visible just because they use the system

These are important parts of making the idea useful, not things we can ignore after launch
_______________________________

➡︎ The part I find most interesting

A person can build a good reputation in one rental business and still have to prove themselves all over again somewhere else

I think that experience can be improved

If someone has spent years renting equipment responsibly, that history should have value beyond the platform or business where it was created

The same should apply to rental businesses

A company that treats customers fairly should have a way to demonstrate that record to people who have never used its service

I'd like to see a system where both sides can build a history, verify previous transactions and use that experience when dealing with new people

That's the idea behind this proposal: 
Rental history that belongs to the people who earn it, and can be verified wherever they need it.
